How it works

  1. Cut-out: a soft matte separates the bottle; label and cap are masked; clear glass is kept as transmittance.
  2. Packshot: the studio sweep is flattened to RGB 255, an even contact shadow and faint reflection are drawn, the label and cap are copied from the photograph with one global exposure change.
  3. Lifestyle: the bottle is staged on an empty scene; Qwen-Image-Edit relights the frame, adds shadow, refraction and reflections.
  4. Lock: the photographed label and cap are placed back on top, aligned to the edit, with only the scene's smooth light applied.
  5. Master: the scene is enlarged three times with Real-ESRGAN and the bottle is placed again from the full-resolution photograph.
  6. Check: every file records the share of photograph pixels, the maximum difference and the label similarity before and after the lock.

Label similarity to the source (SSIM on the label, all 24 locked stills): generator 0.38 to 0.70, after the lock 0.94 to 0.99.

Open-weight models under commercial-use licences: Qwen-Image-2512 and Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (Apache 2.0), BiRefNet (MIT), SAM 2.1 (Apache 2.0), Real-ESRGAN (BSD-3), Qwen3-VL-8B (Apache 2.0).
